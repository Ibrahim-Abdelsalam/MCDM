\section*{\textcolor{critred}{Critical Issues}}

\subsection*{1. Backwards Dates --- 7 rows}
\textbf{Sheet:} \texttt{Relationships}\\
\textbf{Problem:} 7 terminated relationships have \texttt{end\_date} \textbf{before} \texttt{start\_date}, resulting in negative durations (e.g., $-178$ days). This is physically impossible.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
\textbf{relationship\_id} & \textbf{start\_date} & \textbf{end\_date} & \textbf{Duration} \\
\midrule
REL-000041 & 2018-05-15 & 2018-02-09 & \textcolor{critred}{$-95$ days} \\
REL-000111 & 2018-10-15 & 2018-06-24 & \textcolor{critred}{$-113$ days} \\
REL-000115 & 2018-09-18 & 2018-03-24 & \textcolor{critred}{$-178$ days} \\
REL-000161 & 2018-04-23 & 2018-03-11 & \textcolor{critred}{$-43$ days} \\
REL-000288 & 2018-11-29 & 2018-08-27 & \textcolor{critred}{$-94$ days} \\
REL-000290 & 2018-01-26 & 2018-01-17 & \textcolor{critred}{$-9$ days} \\
REL-000298 & 2018-07-19 & 2018-06-29 & \textcolor{critred}{$-20$ days} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Suggested correction:} Swap \texttt{start\_date} and \texttt{end\_date} for each of these 7 rows, or regenerate the \texttt{end\_date} to be after the \texttt{start\_date}.

\bigskip

\subsection*{2. Negative Events with Positive Impact --- 55 rows}
\textbf{Sheet:} \texttt{Events\_Log}\\
\textbf{Problem:} 55 events that are inherently harmful (e.g., \texttt{quality\_incident}, \texttt{financial\_downturn}, \texttt{regulatory\_penalty}, \texttt{supply\_disruption}) have a \textbf{positive} \texttt{impact\_magnitude}, meaning they \emph{improve} the affected criterion score. This is logically backwards.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{llrl}
\toprule
\textbf{entity\_id} & \textbf{event\_type} & \textbf{impact\_mag.} & \textbf{affected\_criterion} \\
\midrule
SUP-00001 & quality\_incident & \textcolor{critred}{+1.39} & Compliance and Sustainability \\
SUP-00010 & regulatory\_penalty & \textcolor{critred}{+0.51} & Technical Capability \\
SUP-00010 & financial\_downturn & \textcolor{critred}{+0.07} & Technical Capability \\
SUP-00013 & quality\_incident & \textcolor{critred}{+0.58} & Experience and Track Record \\
\multicolumn{4}{c}{\emph{(+ 51 more rows)}} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Suggested correction:} Flip the sign of \texttt{impact\_magnitude} to negative for all rows where a harmful event has a positive impact.

\subsection*{4. Supplier \texttt{gate\_status = `fail'} but \texttt{is\_qualified = True} --- 12 rows}
\textbf{Sheet:} \texttt{Supplier\_Ratings}\\
\textbf{Problem:} 12 supplier rating rows show a failed qualification gate (score $< 4.0$) but the supplier is still marked as qualified. Global disqualification rule is violated.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lclccc}
\toprule
\textbf{entity\_id} & \textbf{Year} & \textbf{Criterion} & \textbf{raw\_value} & \textbf{gate} & \textbf{is\_qual} \\
\midrule
SUP-00045 & 2019--2023 & Compliance \& Sustainability & 3.78--3.99 & fail & \textcolor{critred}{True} \\
SUP-00095 & 2018--2023 & Flexibility & 2.98--3.08 & fail & \textcolor{critred}{True} \\
SUP-00094 & 2022 & Flexibility & 4.06 & fail & \textcolor{critred}{True} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Suggested correction:} Change \texttt{is\_qualified} to \texttt{False} for all 12 rows. Also, propagate: all other criteria rows for the same \texttt{entity\_id} + \texttt{year} should also be \texttt{is\_qualified = False}.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Note:} Row for SUP-00094 (\texttt{raw\_value} = 4.06) is a \textbf{double error}: a score of 4.06 should not trigger \texttt{gate\_status = `fail'} at all (threshold is $< 4.0$). Change its \texttt{gate\_status} to \texttt{`pass'}.

\section*{\textcolor{warnyellow}{Significant Issues}}

\subsection*{8. All 345 Relationships Start in 2018 --- No New Relationships in 2019--2023}
\textbf{Sheet:} \texttt{Relationships}\\
\textbf{Problem:} Every single \texttt{start\_date} falls in the year 2018. In a real supply chain spanning 6 years, new partnerships would form each year. This makes the temporal dimension of the relationship data unrealistic for ML training.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Suggested correction:} Distribute relationship \texttt{start\_date} values across 2018--2023 to simulate organic network growth.

\bigskip

\subsection*{9. AHP/Direct Weights Cover Only 3 of 42 Rating Scenarios}
\textbf{Sheet:} \texttt{AHP\_Weights}, \texttt{Direct\_Weights} vs \texttt{Supplier\_Ratings}\\
\textbf{Problem:} The Ratings sheets contain 42 unique scenarios (7 industries $\times$ 6 years). However, AHP and Direct Weight data only exist for 3 scenarios (\texttt{SCN-2023-AUT}, \texttt{SCN-2023-FMC}, \texttt{SCN-2023-BUY}). This means 40 out of 42 scenarios have no corresponding expert weighting data, and only 2 of 7 industries have supplier AHP weights.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Suggested correction:} Generate AHP/Direct Weight data for all 7 industries, and ideally across multiple years to support the time-series ML pipeline.

\bigskip
